## 🌿 A little about me

Hi, I'm **Adrita Chakraborty** — a **Documentation Engineer, Technical Writer and Technical Content Strategist** working at the intersection of **product understanding, technical communication, AI workflows and search discovery**.

I started in the WordPress ecosystem, learning products by testing their features and explaining them to real users. That experience shaped how I write today: **understand the actual workflow first, then document what matters**.

My interests now include **AEO & GEO, semantic content architecture, AI-assisted research, developer-facing SaaS, product QA, and documentation that is both discoverable and genuinely helpful**.

> ✨ My favorite question: **“Would this make sense to someone using the product for the first time?”**

## 🔬 How I approach documentation

<div align="center">

`EXPLORE THE PRODUCT` → `REPRODUCE THE WORKFLOW` → `WRITE CLEAR STEPS` → `VALIDATE` → `IMPROVE`

</div>

| Step | What I look for |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | Who is the reader? What are they trying to accomplish? |
| **Reproduce** | Can I follow the user journey myself and identify prerequisites? |
| **Write** | Are the steps clear, scannable and accurate? |
| **Validate** | Does the documentation match actual product behavior? |
| **Improve** | What feedback or changes should be reflected in the docs? |
